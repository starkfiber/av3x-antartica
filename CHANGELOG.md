# Release notes

## 3.2.0-antartica.6 — public community preview (2026-11-06)

First public repository packaging of the existing V3 line. Adds a portable repo marketplace, MIT license, installation/MCP walkthrough, cited complementary-project comparison, community reporting templates, pinned CI and reviewed release-manifest checks. Original51-topic corpus,13read-onlyMCPtools and30Node regressions are retained. Historical private paths become labelled non-resolving case locators; raw private cases and old machine-specific manifests are excluded.

The SDK-native map starter remains a lab/example with recorded sample search/routes and bounded small offline areas. Fully offline Malaysia-wide shops/search/routing is being developed separately. Reader rendered keyboard/assistive acceptance remains open; this publication does not turn a preview into a completed platform or certify all historical gates.
