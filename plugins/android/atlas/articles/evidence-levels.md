**Evidence level:** derived. This names the main evidence anchor, not universal verification of every explanatory statement.

The central skill is choosing the right evidence for the question. Source explains intended mechanisms at a revision. Shipped bytes reveal the distributed representation. Decompilation makes some of that representation readable. Cryptographic checks establish precisely scoped relationships. Runtime experiments show behavior under specified conditions.

A complete map can still contain unknown territory. Our map covers the major concepts, while full GSI contents, device-specific firmware, native iOS control flow, complete signature chains and actual runtime behavior remain open. Those gaps are recorded, not hidden behind a progress percentage.
